Windows are coarse scheduling bounds. t0 is usually BJD_TDB; when the target's coordinates are known, transit centers are corrected to true UTC before scheduling. Without coordinates, the ≤9 min BJD_TDB/UTC offset falls back to being absorbed by the padding above.

Window boundaries are always rounded to 5-minute intervals. When padding is 0 the rounded window may be slightly shorter than the transit visibility (floor for start, ceil for end). Verify for critical observations.
